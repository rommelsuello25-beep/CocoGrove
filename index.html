About Coco Grove

Luxury Island Escape

Coco Grove Beach Club is one of the most beautiful resorts in Siquijor known for its crystal-clear waters, tropical atmosphere, relaxing villas, and peaceful island experience.

Guests can enjoy white sand beaches, infinity pools, beachfront dining, luxury accommodations, and unforgettable sunsets.

Perfect for family vacations, romantic getaways, and tropical adventures.
